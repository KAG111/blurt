Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

The short version: bowerboard doesn't need you to make an account, and joining a room never asks for your name or any personal details. Room content (words, notes, votes, rankings) is stored against an anonymous room code, not against you.

No accounts, no sign-up

You never need to register, log in, or hand over an email address to join or host a bower. There's nothing to remember and nothing we can match back to "you" as a person from normal use of the app.

What happens when you join or host a room

Everything you post to a room — a word, a sticky note, a vote, a ranking, a brainstorm idea — is stored against that room's code, not against any personal identity. We have no way of knowing who, specifically, posted a given item, unless you choose to type your own name into it.

If you host a room, your browser keeps a private key that proves the room is yours, so you can moderate it or come back to it later. That key stays on your own device and is only ever sent to our database to prove you created that specific room — we don't use it to identify you anywhere else.

Nicknames (quizzes only)

Quiz rooms ask participants to pick a nickname so scores and a leaderboard can work. A nickname is whatever the participant chooses to type — it isn't verified against a real name, email, or any other identity, and the host can remove or edit any nickname before or during the quiz.

What's stored on your own device

A small amount of information is kept only in your own browser's local storage, never sent to us beyond what's described above:

  • A short list of bowers you've recently created, so you can find your way back to them.
  • The private "host key" mentioned above, for any room you've created on that device.

Clearing your browser's site data for bowerboard.app removes all of this.

The contact form

If you choose to use the "Contact Support" form, the email address and message you type are sent to us via a third-party form service (Formspree) so we can reply to you. We only use this to respond to your message — nothing from the contact form is linked to any room you may have used.

Hosting, and the basic logs that come with it

Like almost any website, our hosting provider (Cloudflare) and database provider (Supabase) may automatically log standard technical details as part of normal operation — things like IP address, browser type, and request timestamps. We don't run any analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts of our own, and we don't use this information to build a profile of you.

How long we keep room data

Room content is deleted automatically once a room goes quiet: ordinary bowers after 48 hours of no activity, and demo rooms (the ones used to preview the app) after 2 hours. We don't keep an indefinite archive of what's been posted to a room.

Classrooms and younger participants

Because joining a bower never requires a name, email, or any other personal detail (nicknames aside, which participants make up themselves), it's designed so that students and other participants can take part without entering any personal information at all. If your school, workplace, or organisation has its own data policy requirements, it's worth checking those independently — this page describes what bowerboard itself does, not a certification against any particular law or standard.

Changes to this policy

If anything here changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.

Questions

Reach us any time via the contact form on the homepage.
